# Architecting JalDrishti: Resilient Urban Flood & Heatwave Engine on AWS
**Author:** Bhomesh Razdan | Bharat Builds Tour: Environmental Hacks (Track 02: Heat & Water)

## Overview
Indian metros swing wildly between two hydrological extremes: acute water scarcity and suffocating heatwaves in summer, followed by paralyzed underpasses during monsoon cloudbursts.

JalDrishti AI is a serverless platform built on AWS combining:
1. **Rational Hydrological Runoff Modeling ($Q = C \cdot I \cdot A / 360$)** to predict underpass submergence before commuter vehicles get trapped.
2. **Stull's Wet-Bulb Equation** to forecast fatal human heat stress thresholds.
3. **Fair-Share Tanker Logistics Optimization** to prevent private tanker cartels from exploiting vulnerable informal settlements.
4. **AWS Cedar Zero-Trust Authorization** to secure pump operations and municipal flood declarations.

Built using AWS SAM CLI, Lambda, DynamoDB, EventBridge, and Bedrock, JalDrishti proves that serverless cloud infrastructure can protect lives against accelerating climate volatility.
